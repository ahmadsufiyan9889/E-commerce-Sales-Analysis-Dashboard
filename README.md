# E-commerce-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline

📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report that provides a complete overview of business performance. It enables users to monitor key metrics such as sales, profit, orders, discounts, and quantity sold while exploring trends across categories, regions, products, and customer segments. The dashboard helps business managers and analysts identify growth opportunities and make informed decisions.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Used to design and develop the interactive dashboard.
📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing.
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures, KPIs, and dynamic reports.
🔗 Data Modeling – Established relationships between tables for accurate analysis and filtering.
📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview.
4. Data Source

Source: Sample E-Commerce Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information about customer orders, sales, profit, quantity, discounts, shipping modes, product categories, sub-categories, regions, states, and yearly sales performance. The data was cleaned and transformed before creating the dashboard.

5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate a large amount of sales data every day, making it difficult to identify sales trends, profitable products, high-performing regions, and customer preferences using raw data alone.

Businesses need an interactive dashboard that can provide quick insights into sales performance and support better decision-making.

• Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to:

Monitor overall sales performance.
Analyze profit and order trends.
Compare sales across regions and product categories.
Identify top-selling products and sub-categories.
Support business decisions through interactive visualizations.
• Walkthrough of Key Visuals
📌 KPI Cards

Displays important business metrics:

Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Orders
Average Discount
Total Quantity

These KPIs provide a quick overview of business performance.

🎛 Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard by:

Year
Region
Category
Sub-Category
Segment
Ship Mode

This allows detailed analysis based on different business dimensions.

📊 Sales by Category

A column chart comparing total sales across:

Technology
Furniture
Office Supplies

Helps identify the highest-performing product category.

📈 Sales by Sub-Category

A horizontal bar chart showing sales for different product sub-categories such as Phones, Chairs, Storage, Tables, and Accessories.

This helps identify the most profitable products.

🥧 Sales by Region

A pie chart showing the contribution of each region (West, East, Central, and South) to total sales.

Useful for regional performance comparison.

📉 Sales by Year

A line chart displaying yearly sales trends from 2011 to 2014.

Helps analyze business growth over time.

🗺 State by Region

A map visualization displaying different states grouped by region.

Provides a geographical view of business performance.

💰 Profit by Year

A line chart showing yearly profit trends.

Helps evaluate the company's profitability over time.

🏆 Sales by Product Name

A horizontal bar chart displaying the top-selling products based on total sales.

Helps identify best-performing products.

• Business Impact & Insights
Performance Monitoring: Quickly tracks overall sales, profit, and order performance.
Regional Analysis: Identifies high-performing and low-performing regions.
Product Analysis: Highlights top-selling categories, sub-categories, and products.
Trend Analysis: Monitors yearly sales and profit growth.
Decision Support: Enables business managers to make informed, data-driven decisions using interactive reports.
